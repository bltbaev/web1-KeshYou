Fluffy Kefir Pancakes

Ingredients:

  • Kefir - 250 ml
  • Flour - 200 g
  • Eggs - 2 pieces
  • Sugar - 2 tablespoons
  • Baking soda - 1 teaspoon
  • Salt - a pinch
  • Vegetable oil - for frying

Instructions:

  1. Mix the kefir, eggs, sugar and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add the flour and baking soda and mix until smooth.
  3. Heat a frying pan and add a small amount of oil.
  4. Pour small portions of the batter into the pan.
  5. Cook the pancakes on both sides until golden brown.
  6. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.
