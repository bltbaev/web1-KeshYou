Fluffy Kefir Pancakes

Ingredients:

  • Kefir — 250 ml
  • Flour — 200 g
  • Egg — 1 pc
  • Sugar — 2 tbsp

Instructions:

  1. Whisk the egg with sugar and kefir.
  2. Gradually add flour and mix until smooth.
  3. Fry in a preheated skillet on both sides until golden brown.
