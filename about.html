About Simple Recipes

Simple Recipes is a student project from Astana IT University. We want to make cooking simple, clear and enjoyable for people who are just starting to cook for themselves.

Who it is for

Our website is made for people who want tasty food without long recipes.

Students

Living alone for the first time and want to cook cheap and simple food.

Beginner cooks

Need short ingredient lists and steps that are easy to follow.

Busy families

Look for quick everyday ideas and traditional dishes for the weekend.
