Lunch recipe

Classic borscht

A rich and colorful beet soup with beef and vegetables. Even better the next day.

  • 1 h 30 min
  • 6 servings
  • Medium
More soup ideas

Ingredients

  • Beef - 400 g
  • Beetroot - 1 piece
  • Cabbage - 200 g
  • Potatoes - 3 pieces
  • Carrot - 1 piece
  • Onion - 1 piece
  • Tomato paste - 1 tablespoon
  • Salt, pepper, bay leaf - to taste

Instructions

  1. Simmer the beef in 2.5 liters of water for about 1 hour to make a rich broth.
  2. Chop the onion, grate the carrot and beetroot.
  3. Fry the onion, carrot and beetroot in a pan, then add the tomato paste.
  4. Add diced potatoes and shredded cabbage to the broth and cook for 15 minutes.
  5. Stir in the fried vegetables, add salt, pepper and a bay leaf, and cook for 10 more minutes.
  6. Serve with sour cream and fresh dill.
