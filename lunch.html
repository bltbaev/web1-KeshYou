Classic Borscht

Ingredients:

  • Beef - 400g
  • Beetroot - 1 pc
  • Cabbage - 200g
  • Potatoes - 3 pcs

Instructions:

  1. Simmer the beef to make a rich broth for about 1.5 hours.
  2. Chop the vegetables and sauté the beetroot, onion, and carrots in a pan.
  3. Add potatoes and cabbage to the broth, stir in the sautéed vegetables, and cook until tender.
