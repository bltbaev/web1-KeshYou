Dessert recipe

San Sebastian cheesecake

A creamy Basque cheesecake with a caramelized top. No crust and no water bath needed.

  • 1 h + cooling
  • 8 servings
  • Easy
More desserts

Ingredients

  • Cream cheese - 800 g
  • Sugar - 220 g
  • Eggs - 5 pieces
  • Heavy cream - 400 ml
  • Corn starch - 1 tablespoon
  • Vanilla extract - 1 teaspoon
  • Lemon juice - 1 teaspoon (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 230°C and line a round pan with baking paper.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with sugar until smooth.
  3. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing after each one.
  4. Add the starch and vanilla extract and mix gently.
  5. Pour in the cream and lemon juice and mix until smooth.
  6. Bake for 25-30 minutes until the top is dark, then cool and chill for 4 hours.

FAQ

It is normal: the high temperature caramelizes the sugar and gives the special taste.

Yes, the middle should wobble when it comes out of the oven. It sets in the fridge.

You can use 180 g, the cheesecake will just be less sweet.
