Cheesecake San-Sebastian

Ingredients:

  • Cream cheese - 800g
  • Sugar - 220g
  • Eggs - 5 pcs.
  • Heavy cream - 400ml
  • Corn starch - 1 tablespoon
  • Vanilla extract - 1 tsp.
  • Lemon juice - 1 tsp. (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Beat the cream cheese with sugar until smooth.
  2. Add the eggs one at a time.
  3. Add starch and vanilla extract and mix gently.
  4. Add cream and lemon juice.
  5. Bake for 25-30 minutes at 230°C.
