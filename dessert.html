Cheesecake San-Sebastian

Ingredients:

  • Cream cheese - 800g (room temperature)
  • Sugar - 220g
  • Eggs - 5 pcs. (room temperature)
  • Heavy cream (from 30-33%) - 400ml
  • Corn starch - 1 tablespoon without a slide
  • Vanilla extract - 1 tsp.
  • Lemon juice - 1 tsp. (optional)

Instructions:

  1. In a deep bowl, beat the curd cheese with sugar with a mixer on low speed or with a whisk until smooth.
  2. Add the eggs one at a time, stirring each time until completely combined.
  3. Add starch (or flour) through a sieve and vanilla extract, mix gently.
  4. Pour in the cream and lemon juice, and knead the mixture until smooth and runny.
  5. Bake for 25-30 minutes at 230°C.
