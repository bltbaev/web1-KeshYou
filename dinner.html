Dinner recipe

Traditional beshbarmak

The national Kazakh dish: tender boiled meat with thin homemade noodles and onion sauce.

  • 2 h 30 min
  • 6 servings
  • Medium
More meat dishes

Ingredients

  • Meat (horse meat, beef or lamb) - 1 kg
  • Flour - 300 g
  • Egg - 1 piece
  • Water - 100 ml
  • Onion - 2 pieces
  • Salt, black pepper - to taste

Instructions

  1. Place the meat in a pot with cold water, bring to a boil and remove the foam.
  2. Cook the meat on low heat for about 2 hours, add salt at the end.
  3. Mix the flour, egg, water and a pinch of salt into a firm dough and let it rest for 20 minutes.
  4. Roll out the dough thinly and cut it into squares.
  5. Take out the meat and boil the dough squares in the broth for 3-4 minutes.
  6. Slice the onion, pour hot broth over it with pepper and serve everything on a big plate.
