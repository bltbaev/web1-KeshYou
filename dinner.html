Traditional Beshbarmak

Ingredients:

  • Meat(Horse meat,beef,lamb) - 1.0 kg
  • Dough - 2 slices
  • Onion - 2 pieces
  • Salt, Black pepper - to taste

Instructions:

  1. Place the meat in a pot with water and set the heat to above medium.
  2. Roll out the dough and let it rest.
  3. When the meat is ready, take it out and place the dough in the pot, having first cut it.
  4. When the dough is ready, take it out and serve the dish, placing chopped onion on top.
